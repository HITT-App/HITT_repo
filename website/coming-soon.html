How It Works

From download to dialed-in plan in under 5 minutes.

01

Set your goals

Take a two-minute onboarding quiz. Body composition target, training experience, weekly availability, equipment access, dietary preferences. Be honest — the AI gets better when you are.

02

Let AI build your plan

Workouts, nutrition targets, sleep baselines, and recovery checkpoints are generated in seconds. Every metric is calibrated against your starting point — not someone else's.

03

Track, adapt, transform

Every set, meal, and biometric flows back into the model. Your plan rewrites itself weekly — pushing harder where you're ready, backing off where you're not.
